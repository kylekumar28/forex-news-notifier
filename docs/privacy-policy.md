# TradeFence Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

TradeFence provides economic calendar information and configurable notifications for high-impact financial news events.

## Information We Collect

TradeFence does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, or other personal profile information.

The app stores user notification preferences locally on the device.

## Economic Calendar Data

TradeFence retrieves economic calendar data from a backend service connected to Firebase.

## Notifications

TradeFence requests permission to send notifications so users can receive alerts before and after selected economic news events.

Notification preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

## Third-Party Services

TradeFence may use third-party services including Firebase and Expo-related services required for app functionality and distribution.

These services may process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

## Advertising and Tracking

TradeFence does not currently display advertising and does not intentionally use third-party advertising tracking.

## Data Retention

TradeFence does not currently maintain user accounts or a server-side database of user profiles.

Locally stored app preferences remain on the user's device unless the app is removed or its data is cleared.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as TradeFence develops and new functionality is introduced.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or TradeFence, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the TradeFence project.
